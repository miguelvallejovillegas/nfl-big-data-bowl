# The 3-Cone Finds Pass Rushers. Hoop G Is a Lead When It Is Missing

**Where a defensive lineman ran the 3-cone, that time is the best Combine number we have for his NFL pressure rate. Only 15 of 42 linemen in the 2025 class ran it. For the rest, Hoop G, a number we pull from Run-the-Hoop tracking, is a lead worth a film check, not a rule: about 0.6 pressures a season per standard deviation [90%: −0.4, 1.7], and its clean 2025 test went 0 of 2. The cheapest fix is at the Combine.**

## Why linemen who skip the 3-cone

Pressure is what teams pay pass rushers for, and one stopwatch drill tracks it for linemen. Among rushers with 100+ NFL pass rushes, a faster year-standardized 3-cone goes with a higher pressure rate (ρ = 0.63 [95%: 0.34, 0.83], n = 27; 0.41 after pick, weight, role and NGS athleticism score). That is in-sample on few rushers; held out, no single time survives samples this small (see "How sure we are"). Still, it is the most consistent number we have, and only 36% of the 118 linemen in the 2023–25 classes ran it. Run-the-Hoop is the one lineman drill built around holding a curve at speed. 109 of them ran it, and the Combine's 10 Hz tracking turns that circle into a force reading.

## The ask: three changes at the Combine

1. **Every DL runs the 3-cone.** Where it exists it is the better number; Hoop G is a stand-in.
2. **Record each hoop run's peak sideways g** (the tracking already supports it) and publish it beside the 40.
3. **Time two hoop runs per player.** Single-run reliability is 0.41; two runs raise it to 0.58, so fewer rushers sit in the "film first" middle.

## What Hoop G is

**Hoop G is how hard a lineman can lean into a turn at speed: the sideways force he holds for half a second while circling the hoop.** It is the peak half-second average of acceleration across his path, in g, standardized within Combine year (drill medians shift between years). A typical run peaks at 0.62 g at 3.5 yd/s (7 mph): a circle about 1.8 yards in radius run without slowing, a tight corner rather than a wide loop. One run is a noisy read, so every number below shrinks single-run players toward average.

![Figure 1. A plus call who beat his forecast (Solomon), the 2025 plus call who has not yet (Ezeiruaku) and an interior minus flag (Phillips). Hoop and NFL traces are lined up at their own peaks; one NFL rush is a film cue, not evidence.](figures/fig1_hero.png)

- **Javon Solomon** (EDGE, pick 168, no 3-cone) is the case the rule is built for: 0.73 g in the hoop, then 12.3% pressure against a 6.9% forecast over 211 rushes.
- **Donovan Ezeiruaku** (EDGE, pick 44) has a similar hoop (0.68 g) but a rookie rate of 7.7% against 9.5% over 402 rushes, inside the noise. His strong 3-cone outranks Hoop G anyway.
- **Jordan Phillips** (interior, pick 143, 312 lb) has a low hoop and the slowest 3-cone among 2025 interior linemen who ran one. He is a minus flag, labelled a film check because weight explains part of a low interior hoop. Rookie year: 1.9% against 6.6%.

## If you are a DL coach or scout

The rule is fixed: **a plus call needs the 80% interval of a player's noise-corrected Hoop G to clear zero; a minus flag needs it below zero.** Each rusher is compared with a no-tracking forecast (pick, weight, NFL EDGE share, NGS score, official 10-yd split and 3-cone) fit on the other two draft classes. He "beats" it when the 80% interval of his actual rate sits above it.

| Group, 2023–25 (30+ NFL rushes) | Rushers (2025) | Pressure rate (forecast) | Beat forecast [exact 90%] | Clearly below |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Plus call, EDGE | 7 (1) | 10.0% (7.8%) | 4 of 7 [23–87%] | 0 |
| Plus call, interior | 2 (1) | 8.2% (8.0%) | 0 of 2 [0–78%] | 0 |
| No call, EDGE | 32 (10) | 8.4% (8.2%) | 6 of 32 [8–34%] | 12 |
| No call, interior | 31 (13) | 6.9% (7.2%) | 3 of 31 [3–23%] | 11 |
| Minus flag, interior (film check) | 6 (2) | 4.7% (6.9%) | 0 of 6 [0–39%] | 2 |

EDGE plus calls rushed at 10.0% against a 7.8% forecast and beat it 4 of 7 times, while EDGEs without a call ran at forecast. The two interior plus calls tell us nothing yet. Every minus flag is interior. Those six ran below forecast (4.7% vs 6.9%, none above it), but weight explains part of a low interior hoop, so the flag sends you to film, not down the board.

**For 2026, stated in advance:** the same rule and forecast, scored after the 2027 season on rushers with 30+ rushes. The rule earns its place only if plus calls beat forecast more often than matched rushers without one.

## How sure we are

![Figure 2. Change in held-out explained pressure rate when each measure is added, scored on a draft class the model never saw.](figures/fig2_validation.png)

| Rushers with an NFL rush | Added to pick, weight, role, NGS score | Result | Takeaway |
|---|---|---|---|
| No 3-cone (56) | Hoop G | held out: +11.6 R² pts [90%: −6.8, 32.2] | Leans positive; this is the use case |
| Ran the 3-cone (34) | 3-cone; then Hoop G on top | held out: −11.3 [−34.6, 5.9]; −73.3 [−141.8, −13.1] | Too few rushers to stack terms; never add Hoop G to a 3-cone |
| Ran both, every measure (22) | partial ρ with pressure | in-sample [95%]: | 3-cone 0.33 [−0.17, 0.69]; Hoop G −0.01 [−0.60, 0.55] | The 3-cone outranks Hoop G |

Every test we ran, with the null it tests:

| Test | Null or model | Result [90%] | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plus calls beat forecast more often than other rushers | Hoop G shuffled within class 5,000 times, calls remade | 4 of 9 vs 9 of 69; gap 31 pts | 0.05 |
| Held-out R² gain, 90 rushers, 3 folds | Hoop G shuffled 300 times; screen, fit and forecast all redone | +1.3 pts [−4.7, 8.7] | 0.01 |
| Held-out log score gain, same reruns | same shuffle | +0.95 nats in total | 0.89 |
| Best of the 14 screened drill–outcome pairs | NFL outcomes shuffled within class 2,000 times | Hoop G partial ρ 0.32 | 0.12 |
| Headline effect (count model) | none (posterior) | 0.6 pressures/season [−0.4, 1.7]; P(>0) 0.85 | — |
| 2025 clean test, 0 of 2 | fitted on 2023–24 only | P(0 of 2) 0.52 with the shrunk effect, 0.54 with none | — |
| Hoop G → NFL sideways g at hoop speed | mixed model, player intercept | EDGE 0.002 g [−0.006, 0.011]; interior 0.000 [−0.008, 0.008] | — |

**Why the p-values differ.** The 0.05 tests the calls; the 0.01 tests the held-out R² gain, but the log score, which also charges for overconfident spread, does not survive the same shuffles (0.89). Only the 0.12 prices our search over 14 drill-outcome pairs, so it is the one to quote.

**The matched 19% base rate.** Without a plus call the raw hit rate is 13% (9 of 69). Plus calls are mostly 2023–24 EDGEs, where hits are commoner, so we weight each role-by-class no-call hit rate by the plus calls in that cell: 2 × EDGE 2023 (4/14), 4 × EDGE 2024 (2/8), 1 × EDGE 2025 (0/10), 1 × Int 2023 (1/10), 1 × Int 2025 (0/15).

**Calibration checks.** Scoring Ethan Downs on his 7 rushes, the plus record is 4 of 10 [exact 90%: 15–70%] and the clean class 0 of 3. Forecasting each rusher only from the official times he actually ran, plus calls still beat forecast 4 of 9, against 9 of 69 for everyone else. Rush-weighted, plus calls produced 1.24 times their forecast pressures [90%: 1.09, 1.36], everyone else 0.98; most of that comes from the tuning classes. A model fit on 2023–24 alone gives the 2025 result, 0 of 2, a probability of 0.52, and 0.54 if Hoop G did nothing. Two rookie seasons cannot tell those apart.

![Figure 3. Every scored plus call not in Figure 1 against its out-of-class forecast, with the hoop run that made the call, and rushers without a plus call as pooled base-rate rows.](figures/fig3_calls.png)

The hoop turn itself does not show up in sampled NFL movement for either role (last row of the test table), so the Figure 1 traces are film cues. We cannot say *how* a strong hoop becomes pressure.

## Limitations

- **Not confirmed.** The held-out gain across all rushers spans zero, the best-of-14 screen gives p = 0.12, and a confirmatory test needs about 840 rushers.
- **Small call sample.** 9 scored plus calls, 2 of them clean. The rule and outcome window were set with 2023–24 visible.
- **The 3-cone wins where both exist.** With an observed 3-cone, Hoop G adds about 0.1 pressures a season.
- **Thin NFL data.** Pressure counts ignore rotation, injuries, opponents and scheme; 2025 has one season. The sampled game tracking has about 20 rushes per player and no blockers.
